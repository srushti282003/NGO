Our Impact

Committed to meaningful, measurable social change through five pillars of community development.

OUR APPROACH

How We Create Change

We work at the grassroots level through education, healthcare, skills, welfare and employment. Every initiative is designed with community participation at its core.

INITIATIVE IMPACT

Areas of Impact

Education

Bridging educational gaps through tutoring, learning resources, and mentorship for underprivileged children across communities.

Healthcare

Free health camps, preventive care awareness, and wellness sessions promoting healthier communities.

Skill Development

Practical vocational training equipping individuals with marketable skills for sustainable economic independence.

Social Welfare

Supporting vulnerable groups — women, elderly, and children — through community care and welfare programs.

Employment

Career pathways and livelihood opportunities for sustainable economic independence across communities.

OUR JOURNEY

Activity Timeline

  1. Foundation Established — Abhigam Welfare Foundation began its journey with a commitment to community welfare in Maharashtra.
  2. Education Initiatives — Launched learning support programs for underprivileged children in Thane district.
  3. Healthcare Camps — Organised free health awareness and wellness camps for community members.
  4. Skill Development Programs — Introduced vocational training workshops for women and youth.
  5. Employment Support — Connected trained individuals with livelihood and career opportunities.
