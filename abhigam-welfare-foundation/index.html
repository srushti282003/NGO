WHO WE ARE

About Abhigam Welfare Foundation

Abhigam Welfare Foundation is committed to creating positive social change by supporting communities through education, healthcare, skill development, social welfare and employment opportunities.

Founded with compassion and driven by action, we believe every individual deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, equality, and independence.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities by creating opportunities for education, healthcare, skill development and social welfare. We believe that sustainable change comes through consistent, compassionate action.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive and empowered society where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, equality and independence — regardless of their social or economic background.

OUR COMMITMENT

Creating Change Where It Matters Most

We work at the grassroots level to bring meaningful, lasting change to communities across Maharashtra and beyond.

Education

Supporting access to learning and helping children build brighter futures through tutoring, materials, and mentorship.

Healthcare

Encouraging healthcare awareness, free health camps, and preventive care in underserved communities.

Skill Development

Helping individuals gain practical vocational skills, digital literacy, and greater self-confidence.

Social Welfare

Supporting vulnerable groups — women, elderly, and children — through community care programs.

Employment

Promoting livelihood opportunities, career awareness, and economic independence for families.
