About Abhigam Welfare Foundation

Committed to creating opportunities and strengthening communities across India.

OUR STORY

Who We Are

Abhigam Welfare Foundation is an Indian charitable organisation focused on education, healthcare, skill development, social welfare and employment — supporting communities with compassion and action.

Building Communities
OUR BACKGROUND

Our Story

Abhigam Welfare Foundation was established with a deep commitment to serve underprivileged communities. We work at the grassroots level in Thane, Maharashtra and surrounding areas, addressing the most critical social needs.

Our team of dedicated volunteers and social workers believe that every person — regardless of their socioeconomic background — deserves access to education, healthcare, and meaningful livelihood opportunities.

Join Our Mission

Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities by creating opportunities for education, healthcare, skill development and social welfare — building a foundation for lasting positive change.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive and empowered society where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, equality and independence — regardless of their social or economic background.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Core Values

Compassion

We approach every individual and community with genuine care, empathy and respect — placing people at the heart of everything we do.

Equality

We believe in equal opportunities for all, working to dismantle barriers that prevent individuals from reaching their full potential.

Empowerment

We build confidence and independence through skills, knowledge and community participation — enabling lasting self-sufficiency.

Transparency

We maintain open, honest communication with our donors, volunteers and communities — accountable in every action we take.

Responsibility

We take full responsibility for our programs and their impact, continuously learning and improving to serve communities better.

Community Participation

We believe lasting change comes through togetherness — actively involving communities in designing and implementing solutions.

HOW WE WORK

Our Approach

1. Understand

We deeply understand the unique needs of each community before designing programs.

2. Identify

We identify specific areas where our support can create the most meaningful impact.

3. Participate

We encourage meaningful community participation as active agents of their own change.

4. Sustain

We work towards sustainable, long-term positive change — not just short-term relief.
